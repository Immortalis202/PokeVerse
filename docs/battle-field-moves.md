# Battlefield moves (MOVE-A56)

App 0.99.0 / engine 0.91.0-core / protocol 100. Older encounters keep their saved blocks and these cards stay manual there.

**Fire Pledge.** For 4 rounds (not restarted while active), every grounded combatant on the battlefield, including later entrants, takes the round-end ongoing roll of 2 Fire-type dice, ignoring defenses, through the existing tick controls. Combatants immune to Ground (not grounded) are unaffected.

**Water Pledge.** For 4 rounds, every Chance pool on the battlefield gains 2 dice, exactly where Serene Grace adds its dice.

**Grass Pledge.** Lowers the Dexterity of those it affects by 1 and installs a non-stacking entry hazard on the foes' side: grounded entrants lose 1 Dexterity, like Sticky Web.

**Mist.** For 4 rounds on the user's side, other combatants cannot reduce Attributes; earlier reductions stay. Screen Cleaner removes it. Trait reductions (such as Accuracy losses) are not blocked.

**Lucky Chant.** For the scene on the user's side, one failed die of each Accuracy, reaction and check roll may be rerolled; this is the same reroll a spent Chance grants, and the two do not add. Critical hits grant no bonus dice against these combatants. Damage rolls are not rerolled, and the outside-battle clause is narrative.

**Court Change.** Swaps every side-scoped battlefield rule and entry hazard (force fields, Tailwind, Safeguard, Mist, Lucky Chant, Spikes and similar) between the two sides, keeping their remaining durations. Battle-wide effects stay. Battles with more than two sides need GM adjudication.

Verification references: completion_a56 public-command tests.
