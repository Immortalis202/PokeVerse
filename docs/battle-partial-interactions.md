# Partial battle interactions (milestone 5)

Application **0.29.0**, engine **0.22.0-core**, protocol **30**, database schema **4**.

Milestone 5 reviews all 55 assigned ability entries and adds 67 exact move definitions. It does not claim that all 892 move effects are executable. Unknown cards still require an explicit GM effect resolution; excluded narrative effects, lethal wounds, automatic Will recovery and trainer-target damage remain excluded. Remaining medicine and held-item definitions belong to milestone 6.

## Reviewed changes

- **Unseen Fist:** non-ranged Physical moves bypass shields, their retaliation, force fields, decoys and Cover. Ranged Physical and Special moves retain protection. Teravolt/Turboblaze also bypass force fields, Cover and non-unique forced-switch prevention. Conflicting unique abilities remain explicit GM rulings.
- **Switching:** Guard Dog prevents foe Switcher moves. Suction Cups remains supported; Teravolt/Turboblaze bypass these non-unique protections. Parting Shot uses sourced stat changes. User Switcher moves respect Arena Trap, Magnet Pull and Shadow Tag eligibility and range. The cards do not specify whether external forced replacement takes precedence over trapping: retain an explicit GM switching exception instead of importing video-game precedence.
- **Timing:** the GM can record printed or ability timing for Mycelium Might in the existing targeting controls. Both choices retain Never Miss. Ability timing removes printed Reaction and uses Late Reaction 1; printed timing retains the card's timing. Existing Prankster/Gale Wings/Triage choices remain. Charge reactions other than Clash still require an explicit GM ruling because preparation/release timing is unspecified; granting priority does not invent a one-action charge.
- **Stat effects:** 44 exact definitions in `rules/battle-interactions.json` cover ordinary attribute/defense changes and three trait cards. Protection, reflection, reversal, once-only Competitive/Defiant retaliation and non-stacking move channels reuse the authoritative sourced-change path. Multi-clause and random selector cards are not inferred from structured JSON alone.
- **Traits:** Coil and Hone Claws grant one accuracy-pool die; Focus Energy grants High Critical for damaging moves. Costar and Opportunist copy those positive move traits separately from attributes/defenses, using immutable snapshots. Repeated copies do not stack. Accuracy successes, Evasion allowances, weight/height and undefined trait formulas are distinct and remain unsupported unless another reviewed definition handles them. Baton Pass retains its printed attribute-only transfer scope. No permanent sheet is edited.
- **Chance/status:** 23 exact additional definitions use the printed chance count and Burn degree/status. Serene Grace adds two dice; Wonder Skin removes up to two. Shield Dust blocks incoming added effects unless bypassed; status immunities, Synchronize and Corrosion use the existing sourced condition path. Sheer Force's chosen damage mode removes these added effects. Independent selectors are not chance dice. Multi-effect cards remain explicit until reviewed.
- **Recoil/healing:** all supplied Recoil tags are boolean and use the existing damage-success recoil rule. Rock Head and Magic Guard in either As One slot now reserve zero recoil dice, leaving chance dice aligned. Explicit self-damage costs remain distinct. Heal Pulse is the sole supplied healing Pulse card: Mega Launcher already upgrades it to Complete Heal with one Will and the existing healing limits/restrictions. Other healing/cure cards retain their individual definitions.
- **Weather/terrain:** remove obsolete booster-item dependencies from Hadron Engine, Orichalcum Pulse, Protosynthesis and Quark Drive. Their supplied cards define weather/terrain activation; tied highest attributes retain the existing owner choice. No Booster Energy behavior is fabricated.

## Evidence and encounter safety

The owned move registry records exact source filenames, SHA-256 fingerprints and supplied effect text. Source data is unchanged. Catalog checks enforce those fingerprints and effect wording. Ability pages and scopes remain in `rules/ability-coverage.json`; counts are **242 automated, 60 partial, 3 manual**. Automated means the stated reviewed scope.

New battles save `interaction_rules`; missing state defaults to false, so existing saved encounters preserve prior behavior. Upgrade the campaign between battles. The prior standalone, information, form, item and event engine gates remain cumulative. The new flag travels in normal campaign snapshots, receipts, reconnects, archives and rollback checkpoints. Timing choices are existing reasoned GM-only Tactics commands, blocked after damage commits. Standard host revision/retry validation applies. Schema 4 is unchanged; every room client must use protocol 30.

Behavior fixtures cover full attack-to-effect resolution for chance/status, every owned stat/trait definition, protection and reflection, copied traits without sheet mutation or stacking, forced switches, GM permissions, timing persistence and rollback, secondary recoil dice and historical gates. The full room suite remains part of release validation. Native clicks, installed upgrades/data preservation and physical LAN acceptance remain milestone 7 and are not claimed here.
