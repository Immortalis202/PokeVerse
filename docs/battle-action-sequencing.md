# Reviewed action sequencing

Application 0.22.0, engine 0.15.0-core, protocol 23; database schema remains 4. Sources are the supplied Pokerole 3.0 Dancer, Parental Bond, Gorilla Tactics, Truant and Defeatist ability cards and their MASTER page references in the owned ability registry. Supplied data remains unchanged.

## Printed rules and implementation

- **Dancer:** once per round, gain a free action to copy a Move containing the word Dance, immediately after the source attempt, with the same Accuracy successes and fresh damage. The owner may decline; a later source attempt remains eligible until a free copy is used. The card says a Pokemon, so the holder's own Dance attempts qualify too. Copies do not spend an action or require the Move to be learned; the used Move is still recorded. Sleep, ice, Flinch, Confusion checks, charge state, Disable, normal resources, move blockers, rank, range and target validation remain binding. Self, single-target, field and Area dances use the existing target planner. Random Foe choices require the GM, while the owner may decline. Multiple Dancers resolve in stable registration order; accepted copies keep that order. Rampage dances are copied once without starting a new rampage. Ordinary copied move effects resolve through their existing definitions; unsupported added effects still require explicit GM adjudication.
- **Parental Bond:** every executable damage pool is rolled twice, and the higher success count deals damage once. This is not a second hit. Costs, shields, HP updates, recoil, decoys and hit callbacks execute once. Shared-target attacks retain both independent dice lanes; each defense uses the appropriate prefix from each lane, and may choose a different winning lane. Ties keep the first pool. Fixed damage rolls no dice and is unchanged. Attack snapshots retain the relevant ability identity after same-hit changes.
- **Gorilla Tactics:** the owner chooses one currently learned Move on entry or effective activation. That Move may be attempted up to five times per round within the normal five-action budget. Other Moves and non-Move battle maneuvers are blocked by the printed only-that-Move restriction; holding and trainer-directed switching remain available. Strength +1 uses the existing non-stacking ability channel. Withdrawal, fainting or effective identity change removes the selection and requires a new choice on return. A sheet with no learned Move can hold or withdraw; no arbitrary Move is invented. A generic manual acknowledgment cannot substitute for an explicit move choice.
- **Truant:** count the holder's personal initiative turns across rounds. The first turn acts normally; every second turn includes a Loyalty roll requiring two successes with the declared action. Failure forfeits that turn, without spending the refused action, using its Move or holding the Pokemon for the whole round. Success permits normal validation. A successful check is retained for the turn, including successive actions committed together. Out-of-turn reactions keep normal reaction rules. Entry or effective ability activation starts a fresh personal-turn count. This uses Pokerole turns, not an invented alternating-round video-game rule.
- **Defeatist:** at half HP or less (including odd maximum HP), every declared action receives a separate Loyalty roll requiring two successes. Failure lowers Strength and Special by two for that action, with the normal attribute floor. The result affects the action's Accuracy and frozen damage inputs, then ends; it never modifies the permanent sheet or carries into another action. Reactions, recovery actions, ice attempts, charge releases and each committed successive action receive their own checks. A charge's release is a new action and does not reuse the preparation result. Healing or an ability change after the action begins does not rewrite its frozen input.

## Host rolls, decisions and limits

Loyalty is read from a reviewed CharacterBuild and must be 0-5; missing legacy Loyalty requires an explicit sheet review. Zero Loyalty fails deterministically. Checks are part of the declared command, rather than rerollable preparation commands. The host reserves an unpenalized action pool and a separate Loyalty prefix, then uses the appropriate action dice after the check. Unused reserved dice are discarded, never saved or reused. A failed Truant declaration is still validated before forfeiting the turn. Normal pain, accuracy, resource, action and target rules apply to the actual action; the literal Loyalty check requires two successes without importing a video-game substitute.

Previews show the Loyalty check and reserved dice, Gorilla's restriction and the two Parental Bond pools. The host enforces bounded pool limits: ordinary commands retain 100, Loyalty-prefixed actions allow 105, Parental Bond allows 200, and explicit five-action successive declarations allow 525. These limits accommodate independently validated component pools rather than allowing arbitrary oversized rolls.

Entry and Dancer choices use the existing owner/GM event interface. A distinct host is not automatically the GM or either owner. Random Foe selections are GM-only; receipt/revision validation prevents repeated choices or dice. Sequencing history, pending copied attempts, chosen Moves, per-round use, personal-turn counts and both damage lanes serialize in encounter snapshots and restore through rollback/reconnect. Ending an encounter clears live sequencing state and retains history. Earlier engines keep their flags and single-pool behavior.

## Verification

The sequencing fixtures cover complete owner choices, five-repeat limits, per-action and successive Loyalty checks, reaction/charge lifetimes, both shared damage lanes, Dancer self/field/rampage/missed copies, rollback, serialization and historical defaults. A separate host, GM and two-owner WebSocket fixture covers ownership, retries, stale commands, persistence and reconnect. Validation passed 417 Rust tests, 33 frontend tests, 12 catalog checks, production build, formatting and warnings-denied Clippy. Windows NSIS packaging produced `PokeVerse_0.22.0_x64-setup.exe` (213.74 MiB); the executable and installer were not launched. Native interaction and physical LAN remain separate acceptance work.
